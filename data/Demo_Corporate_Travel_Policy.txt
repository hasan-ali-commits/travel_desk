DEMO CORPORATE TRAVEL POLICY
================================

Document Type: Internal Corporate Policy
Policy Owner: Human Resources and Administration
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 1 January 2026
Review Cycle: Annual
Applicability: All employees and approved contractors

IMPORTANT
---------
This is a fictional demonstration policy created for a Retrieval-Augmented
Generation (RAG) chatbot workshop. It is not a real company's policy and should
not be used to approve actual travel or expenses.

1. PURPOSE
----------
This policy defines the rules for requesting, approving, booking, undertaking,
and claiming expenses for business travel. It aims to ensure employee safety,
cost control, consistent approvals, and transparent expense reimbursement.

2. SCOPE
--------
This policy applies to employees travelling on approved business for:
- Client meetings and workshops
- Conferences, seminars, and training
- Project delivery and implementation
- Internal business meetings
- Other activities approved by the employee's manager

Personal travel, daily commuting, and travel undertaken without business
approval are outside the scope of this policy.

3. TRAVEL REQUEST AND APPROVAL
------------------------------
3.1 Employees must submit a travel request through the company's travel portal
or the designated travel desk before making any booking.

3.2 The request should include:
- Business purpose and destination
- Proposed departure and return dates
- Expected travel and accommodation costs
- Project, client, or cost-centre details
- Any special accessibility or safety requirements

3.3 The employee's reporting manager must approve the business purpose and
budget before booking.

3.4 International travel requires approval from the reporting manager and the
Business Unit Head. Finance approval is also required when the estimated cost
exceeds the approved project or department budget.

3.5 Employees must not approve their own travel requests or expense claims.

4. BOOKING GUIDELINES
---------------------
4.1 All air, rail, and hotel bookings should be made through the authorised
travel desk or approved booking channel.

4.2 Employees should book at least:
- Domestic travel: 7 calendar days in advance, where practical
- International travel: 21 calendar days in advance, where practical

4.3 Economy class is the standard class for domestic and international flights.
Any exception requires prior written approval from the Business Unit Head.

4.4 Employees should select the lowest logical fare that meets business
requirements. A reasonable difference in price may be accepted for safer
connections, practical arrival times, or reduced overall journey time.

4.5 Flight bookings should include reasonable connection times. Employees
should avoid unnecessary stopovers and premium add-ons.

4.6 Rail travel should use the class approved for the employee's grade or
business unit. If no grade-based entitlement is published, the reporting
manager must approve the class before booking.

4.7 Employees must not split bookings or change travel details to bypass
approval limits.

5. HOTEL AND ACCOMMODATION
--------------------------
5.1 Employees should use company-preferred hotels where available.

5.2 The following nightly limits are illustrative maximums for this demo policy:

- Tier 1 cities (for example, Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai):
  INR 6,000 per night, excluding applicable taxes
- Tier 2 cities: INR 4,500 per night, excluding applicable taxes
- Other locations: INR 3,500 per night, excluding applicable taxes

5.3 A higher limit may be approved when preferred hotels are unavailable,
during major events, or when safety and location requirements justify the cost.
Prior written approval from the reporting manager and Finance is required.

5.4 Hotel bills must show the employee's name, stay dates, and itemised charges
where available.

5.5 Personal entertainment, minibar purchases, spa services, and personal
guest expenses are not reimbursable.

6. LOCAL TRANSPORTATION
-----------------------
6.1 Employees should use safe, economical transportation appropriate to the
location and business schedule.

6.2 Airport transfers may be booked through the approved travel desk or claimed
with a valid receipt.

6.3 App-based taxis, metered taxis, and public transportation are reimbursable
when used for approved business travel.

6.4 Rental cars require prior manager approval. The employee must hold a valid
driving licence and comply with local traffic and insurance requirements.

6.5 Fuel, tolls, and parking charges for an approved rental or personal vehicle
may be reimbursed when supported by receipts and business-travel details.

7. MEALS AND INCIDENTAL EXPENSES
--------------------------------
7.1 Reasonable meal expenses during approved business travel are reimbursable
within the following illustrative daily limits:

- Domestic travel: INR 1,200 per day
- International travel: USD 40 per day, or the locally approved equivalent

7.2 Meal claims must be supported by itemised receipts where available.

7.3 Alcohol, tobacco, personal shopping, and expenses for family members or
other unauthorised guests are not reimbursable.

7.4 Tips are reimbursable only when customary, reasonable, and permitted by
local rules.

7.5 Employees must not claim a meal expense if the same meal was provided and
paid for by the company, event organiser, or client.

8. TRAVEL ADVANCES
------------------
8.1 Employees may request a travel advance for approved business travel when
needed.

8.2 The advance request must be approved by the reporting manager and Finance.

8.3 Employees must reconcile the advance through an expense claim within
7 calendar days after completing the trip.

8.4 Any unused advance must be returned to the company within the same
7-calendar-day period.

9. EXPENSE CLAIMS AND REIMBURSEMENT
-----------------------------------
9.1 Employees must submit expense claims within 10 calendar days after
completing the trip.

9.2 Each claim must include:
- Approved travel request or reference number
- Dates and business purpose of travel
- Itemised expense details and currency
- Original or digital receipts, where applicable
- Proof of payment when requested
- Project or cost-centre information

9.3 Claims must be accurate, complete, and submitted by the employee who
incurred the expense.

9.4 Finance will review claims for policy compliance and may request
clarification or additional documents.

9.5 Approved claims will normally be reimbursed within 15 business days after
Finance receives a complete and compliant submission.

9.6 Claims submitted after the deadline may be rejected unless the employee
provides a valid reason and obtains an exception approval.

10. CANCELLATIONS AND CHANGES
-----------------------------
10.1 Employees must notify the travel desk and reporting manager as soon as a
business trip is cancelled or changed.

10.2 Refundable or changeable fares should be preferred when the business
schedule is uncertain and the price difference is reasonable.

10.3 Cancellation charges caused by a business requirement or circumstances
outside the employee's control may be reimbursed with manager approval.

10.4 Charges caused by an employee's personal preference or failure to follow
the approved itinerary may not be reimbursed.

10.5 Any unused tickets, hotel credits, or refunds must be reported to the
travel desk and must not be retained for personal use.

11. INTERNATIONAL TRAVEL
------------------------
11.1 International travel requires the approvals described in Section 3.4.

11.2 Employees are responsible for providing accurate passport details and
supporting documents in time for visa processing.

11.3 Visa fees, mandatory business-travel insurance, and required vaccinations
may be reimbursed when approved in advance and supported by receipts.

11.4 Employees must comply with immigration, customs, local laws, and company
security requirements.

11.5 Employees should review destination-specific health, safety, and security
guidance before departure.

12. HEALTH, SAFETY, AND SECURITY
--------------------------------
12.1 Employee safety takes priority over cost savings.

12.2 Employees should use reputable transport providers and avoid unsafe
locations or travel arrangements.

12.3 Employees must promptly report accidents, illness, lost travel documents,
security incidents, or other emergencies to their manager and the designated
company contact.

12.4 Employees travelling internationally should keep emergency contact
details and copies of essential travel documents accessible.

12.5 Employees must follow applicable company travel-security instructions,
including device and data-protection requirements.

13. NON-REIMBURSABLE EXPENSES
-----------------------------
The following expenses are not reimbursable unless a specific written
exception is approved:
- Personal holidays, sightseeing, and leisure activities
- Expenses for family members or personal guests
- Alcohol and tobacco
- Personal clothing, gifts, and shopping
- Traffic fines and parking penalties
- Lost personal items
- Hotel movies, gaming, spa, and minibar charges
- Duplicate claims or expenses already paid by another party
- Expenses without adequate supporting documentation, where documentation is
  required

14. POLICY EXCEPTIONS
---------------------
14.1 Exceptions must be requested in writing before the expense is incurred,
where practical.

14.2 The reporting manager and the relevant Business Unit Head must approve
exceptions. Finance approval is required for budget or reimbursement
exceptions.

14.3 An exception approval must state the business reason, estimated cost, and
scope of the exception.

14.4 Verbal approval alone is not sufficient evidence for reimbursement.

15. NON-COMPLIANCE
------------------
15.1 Claims that are inaccurate, duplicated, unsupported, or inconsistent
with this policy may be rejected.

15.2 Suspected misuse of company funds may be referred to HR, Finance, or the
appropriate internal investigation team.

15.3 Deliberate misrepresentation may result in disciplinary action in
accordance with company procedures.

16. CONTACT AND SUPPORT
-----------------------
For travel bookings, contact the authorised Travel Desk.
For policy interpretation, contact Human Resources.
For expense claims, reimbursements, and advances, contact Finance.

Employees should include their employee ID, travel request number, trip dates,
and a brief description of the issue when raising a support request.
